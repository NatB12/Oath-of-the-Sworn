# Oath of the Sworn

A dark fantasy, choose-your-own-adventure RPG that runs in the browser, built in HTML. You play a knight who swore an oath to their order as the kingdom falls apart, and every choice you make pushes the story down a different path.

> An exiled warrior ventures into the ruins of a broken world, uncovering forgotten truths hidden beneath a kingdom suspended in the sky.

## Overview

Oath of the Sworn combines branching narrative with turn-based combat. You choose your name and class, then work through a story where mercy, loyalty and duty are in tension. Fights are short and tactical, and what you do after them often matters more than whether you win.

## Features

- **Branching story:** Decisions shape how events unfold, including whether to spare, rob or question the enemies you defeat.
- **Turn-based combat:** Manage a small pool of resources and pick abilities that fit your class.
- **Three classes:** Each has its own resource and playstyle.
- **Moral choices:** Rival visions of how to save a failing kingdom pull at your character.
- **Browser-based:** No install needed. Open it and play.

## Characters

- **The Player:** A knight bound by an oath to their order. You always carry a sword and an oath token.
- **Elara:** A compassionate reformer who believes the kingdom can be rebuilt through mercy. Her crown and signet ring grant access to power.
- **Sir Garran:** An uncompromising zealot devoted to the old order. His sword and seal mark his authority.

## Classes

## Playtest Scenario

The opening encounter shows how the game fits together. You make camp in the woods and are ambushed by a Bladesworn bandit with 8 HP, while you have 10 HP. When the bandit falls to 0 HP, they surrender, and you choose their fate:

1. **Execute** them.
2. **Take their weapons and gold** and let them go.
3. **Interrogate** them, which reveals a deserter from your own order.
